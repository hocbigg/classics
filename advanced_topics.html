Advanced Topics

Learners are encouraged to select one or two tracks aligned with their interests rather than attempting to complete all tracks.

  • Classical Philology, Palaeography & Textual Criticism: Investigates how ancient texts were copied, corrupted, edited, and reconstructed from manuscript traditions, papyrological remains, and ancient scholia.
  • Ancient Philosophy, Science & Intellectual History: Explores Greco-Roman metaphysics, epistemology, ethics, natural philosophy, and medicine from the Presocratics through Hellenistic and Imperial philosophy.
  • Classical Archaeology, Epigraphy & Material Culture: Analyzes non-literary evidence through landscape field survey, urban domestic space, ancient numismatics, and stone inscriptions.
  • Late Antiquity & Byzantine Transitions: Traces the political, religious, and institutional evolution of the Mediterranean from the Third Century Crisis through early Byzantium.
  • Classical Reception, Cultural Politics & Gender Studies: Analyzes how classical antiquity has been interpreted, reimagined, ideologically deployed, and critically interrogated regarding gender, sexuality, and imperial hegemony.
  • Digital Classics & Computational Humanities: Trains learners in computational linguistics, natural language processing, semantic markup, and geospatial technologies applied to ancient sources.

Classical Philology, Palaeography & Textual Criticism

Investigates how ancient texts were copied, corrupted, edited, and reconstructed from manuscript traditions, papyrological remains, and ancient scholia.

History of Textual Transmission: Scribes and Scholars: A Guide to the Transmission of Greek and Latin Literature (L. D. Reynolds and N. G. Wilson) - Traces the physical and cultural transmission of ancient Greek and Latin literature from classical antiquity through the Byzantine and Western Middle Ages to the Renaissance.

Textual Criticism & Editorial Technique: Textual Criticism and Editorial Technique Applicable to Greek and Latin Texts (Martin L. West) - The definitive methodological manual on constructing stemmata codicum, identifying scribal errors, and evaluating conjectural emendations.

Latin Palaeography & Manuscript Studies: Latin Palaeography: Antiquity and the Middle Ages (Bernhard Bischoff) - The canonical handbook on Latin script classifications, codicology, and scribal abbreviation systems from the Roman Empire onward.

Latin Palaeography & Manuscript Studies: Reading Old Documents: Latin Palaeography (The National Archives) - A free interactive tutorial providing practical transcription exercises, letterform charts, and abbreviation decoding on archival Latin manuscripts.

Documentary & Literary Papyrology: The Oxford Handbook of Papyrology (Roger S. Bagnall, ed.) - A comprehensive guide to deciphering, conserving, and interpreting Greek and Latin documentary records and literary fragments from Greco-Roman Egypt.

Ancient Scholarship & Commentary Traditions: Ancient Greek Scholarship (Eleanor Dickey) - A practical, step-by-step guide to reading, navigating, and translating ancient and Byzantine marginal scholia, lexica, and grammatical treatises.

Ancient Philosophy, Science & Intellectual History

Explores Greco-Roman metaphysics, epistemology, ethics, natural philosophy, and medicine from the Presocratics through Hellenistic and Imperial philosophy.

Presocratic and Platonic Philosophy: Ancient Philosophy: Plato and His Predecessors (Coursera/University of Pennsylvania/Susan Sauvé Meyer) - A structured online course covering the Milesian naturalists, Parmenidean ontology, and Plato's epistemology, virtue ethics, and theory of Forms.

Presocratic and Platonic Philosophy: The Presocratic Philosophers: A Critical History with a Selection of Texts (G. S. Kirk, J. E. Raven, and M. Schofield) - The benchmark critical edition and commentary analyzing surviving philosophical fragments and early Greek cosmologies from Thales through the atomists.

Aristotelian and Hellenistic Philosophy: Ancient Philosophy: Aristotle and His Successors (Coursera/University of Pennsylvania/Susan Sauvé Meyer) - An advanced university course unpacking Aristotle's Categories, natural teleology, and virtue ethics alongside Epicurean and Stoic philosophical systems.

Aristotelian and Hellenistic Philosophy: The Hellenistic Philosophers: Volume 1, Translations of the Principal Sources with Philosophical Commentary (A. A. Long and D. N. Sedley) - The primary scholarly sourcebook providing English translations and analytical commentary arranged systematically by Hellenistic philosophical doctrine.

Ancient Medicine and Humoral Physiology: Ancient Medicine (Vivian Nutton) - The standard comprehensive history of clinical practice, anatomical dissection, pharmacology, and humoral theory from the Hippocratic corpus to Galen.

Classical Archaeology, Epigraphy & Material Culture

Analyzes non-literary evidence through landscape field survey, urban domestic space, ancient numismatics, and stone inscriptions.

Mediterranean Landscape Archaeology: Side-by-Side Survey: Comparative Regional Studies in the Mediterranean World (Susan E. Alcock and John F. Cherry, eds.) - An advanced methodological volume examining surface artifact scatters, regional demographic modeling, and field survey comparison across the Mediterranean.

Roman Urban Topography and Field Stratigraphy: The Changing Landscape of Ancient Rome: Archaeology and History of the Palatine Hill (Coursera/Sapienza University of Rome/Paolo Carafa) - A university video course covering urban archaeological investigation, stratigraphic analysis, and architectural reconstruction using Rome's Palatine Hill as a primary case study.

Roman Domestic Space and Spatial Syntax: Houses and Society in Pompeii and Herculaneum (Andrew Wallace-Hadrill) - A landmark study examining how architectural spatial syntax, wall decoration, and domestic layout negotiated Roman social status, patronage, and privacy.

Greek and Roman Numismatics: Ancient History from Coins (Christopher Howgego) - A foundational guide to using coin hoards, minting patterns, and iconography to reconstruct state finance, monetization, and imperial propaganda.

Epigraphic Methodology and Inscriptional Evidence: The Cambridge Manual of Latin Epigraphy (Alison E. Cooley) - An exhaustive technical handbook on epigraphic abbreviations, monument classifications, dating formulas, and the civic function of public writing.

Late Antiquity & Byzantine Transitions

Traces the political, religious, and institutional evolution of the Mediterranean from the Third Century Crisis through early Byzantium.

Transformation of the Roman Mediterranean: The Early Middle Ages, 284–1000 (Open Yale Courses/Paul Freedman) - A complete 22-lecture university course detailing the Diocletianic reforms, Christianization, barbarian kingdoms, Justinianic reconquest, and Byzantine survival.

Transformation of the Roman Mediterranean: The World of Late Antiquity: AD 150–750 (Peter Brown) - The landmark monograph establishing Late Antiquity as an era of vibrant religious innovation and cultural reconfiguration rather than catastrophic decline.

Patristic Literature and Philosophical Autobiography: The Confessions of Augustine: Electronic Edition and Commentary (James J. O'Donnell) - An exhaustive, open-access digital philological commentary on Augustine's psychological, rhetorical, and theological masterwork.

Neoplatonism and Late Pagan Metaphysics: The Cambridge Companion to Plotinus (Lloyd P. Gerson, ed.) - An advanced analysis of Plotinian hypostases, the contemplative ascent of the soul, and the philosophical synthesis of late antique Platonism.

Imperial Continuity and the Eastern Roman State: The Byzantine Republic: People and Power in New Rome (Anthony Kaldellis) - Reconstructs the civic republican ideology, public consensus mechanisms, and popular sovereignty that persisted in medieval Roman Constantinople.

Classical Reception, Cultural Politics & Gender Studies

Analyzes how classical antiquity has been interpreted, reimagined, ideologically deployed, and critically interrogated regarding gender, sexuality, and imperial hegemony.

Hermeneutics and Reception Theory: Redeeming the Text: Latin Poetry and the Hermeneutics of Reception (Charles Martindale) - Demonstrates that the meaning of ancient literature is inextricably shaped by history, post-antique translations, and shifting interpretive horizons.

Performance Reception and Ancient Drama: Archive of Performances of Greek and Roman Drama (University of Oxford/APGRD) - An open-access multimedia research hub and lecture archive documenting modern theatrical, operatic, and cinematic stagings of Greek and Roman drama.

Gender, Sexuality, and Ancient Bodily Norms: Sexuality in Greek and Roman Culture (Marilyn B. Skinner) - An advanced social history examining Greek pederasty, Roman citizen norms, female subcultures, and ancient constructions of gendered embodiment.

Ethnicity, Race, and Ancient Imperialism: Race: Antiquity and Its Legacy (Denise Eileen McCoskey) - A critical study contrasting ancient environmental and cultural theories of difference with modern racial discourses and classical appropriations.

History of Classical Scholarship and Imperial Education: Classics Transformed: Schools, Universities, and Society in England, 1830–1960 (Christopher Stray) - Traces the sociological evolution of classical philology into an elite educational apparatus linked to social class and British imperial administration.

Digital Classics & Computational Humanities

Trains learners in computational linguistics, natural language processing, semantic markup, and geospatial technologies applied to ancient sources.

Digital Text Encoding and Epigraphic Markup: Sunoikisis Digital Classics (SunoikisisDC Consortium) - An open-access international training program offering video seminars, syllabi, and hands-on exercises in EpiDoc TEI XML, treebanking, and digital philology.

Natural Language Processing for Historical Languages: The Classical Language Toolkit (CLTK Documentation) - An open-source Python library and documentation providing specialized NLP pipelines for lemmatization, part-of-speech tagging, and text analysis across ancient languages.

Spatial Humanities and Archaeological GIS: Geographical Information Systems in Archaeology (James Conolly and Mark Lake) - A foundational guide to applying spatial analysis, digital elevation modeling, and landscape GIS methodologies to ancient Mediterranean archaeological contexts.

Methodological Foundations of Digital Classics: The Oxford Handbook of Digital Classical Studies (Elton Barker, Olympia Bobou, and Rubina Raja, eds.) - An authoritative survey exploring digital scholarship across classical archaeology, computational linguistics, 3D modeling, and linked open data.
