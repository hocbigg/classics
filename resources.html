Resources

Primary Text Corpora & Digital Readers

Searchable digital collections and online reading environments for ancient Greek and Latin literature.

  • Perseus Digital Library (Tufts University) - The landmark open-access digital repository of Greco-Roman primary literature, featuring linked morphological parsing, critical apparatuses, and facing translations.
  • PHI Latin Texts (Packard Humanities Institute) - A fast, comprehensive concordance and search corpus containing virtually all surviving classical Latin literature written before 200 CE.
  • The Scaife Viewer (Perseus Digital Library) - A modernized, modular reading environment designed for reading and comparing ancient Greek and Latin texts with standardized CTS passage citation.
  • The Latin Library - A clean, unencumbered collection of unannotated Latin texts spanning classical antiquity, the Middle Ages, and the neo-Latin era.
  • The Chicago Homer (University of Chicago) - A searchable, multilingual reading database dedicated to early Greek epic (Homer and Hesiod) that tracks formulaic repetitions and syntactic structures.

Lexicons, Dictionaries & Morphological Analyzers

Digital lookup tools, inflection engines, and customizable vocabulary builders for classical Greek and Latin.

  • Logeion (University of Chicago) - A centralized dictionary search engine that simultaneously queries Liddell-Scott-Jones (LSJ), Lewis & Short, Middle Liddell, and the Diccionario Griego-Español alongside morphological parsing.
  • The Bridge (Haverford College) - A customizable vocabulary list generator that filters and exports targeted Greek and Latin frequency lists based on specific authors, texts, or textbook mastery.
  • Dickinson College Commentaries (Dickinson College) - An open hub featuring vetted core vocabulary frequency lists (the 1,000 most common Latin words and 500 most common Greek words) essential for building sight-reading speed.
  • William Whitaker's Words (University of Notre Dame Archives) - A rapid, rule-based morphological analyzer and dictionary that identifies obscure inflected forms, enclitics, prefixes, and stems in Latin.

Epigraphy, Papyrology & Inscriptional Databases

Searchable corpora of documentary evidence preserved on papyrus, stone, bronze, and wooden writing tablets.

  • Papyri.info (Duke University Libraries) - The primary open-access portal for documentary papyrology, integrating the Duke Databank of Documentary Papyri, the Heidelberger Gesamtverzeichnis, and museum inventory records.
  • Searchable Greek Inscriptions (Packard Humanities Institute) - The largest searchable open repository of ancient Greek inscriptions, containing over 210,000 published texts catalogued by geographic region and standard epigraphic corpus.
  • Epigraphik-Datenbank Clauss / Slaby (EDCS) - An exhaustive database indexing over 500,000 Latin and bilingual inscriptions from across the Roman world with publication citations and epigraphic abbreviations.
  • Roman Inscriptions of Britain (RIB Online) - A fully searchable, open digital edition of monumental stone inscriptions and ink writing tablets (including the Vindolanda Tablets) discovered in Roman Britain.

Historical Geography, Maps & Spatial Modeling

Interactive gazetteers, topographical atlases, and spatial simulation engines for the ancient Mediterranean.

  • Pleiades (Stoa Consortium / NYU ISAW) - The authoritative linked open data gazetteer of the ancient world, supplying persistent URIs, historical names, and coordinates for thousands of ancient locations.
  • ToposText (Aikaterini Laskaridis Foundation) - An interactive map and gazetteer that connects over 8,000 ancient Mediterranean locations and archaeological ruins directly to excerpts from ancient literature mentioning them.
  • Digital Atlas of the Roman Empire (Johan Åhlfeldt / University of Gothenburg) - A detailed interactive vector map of the Roman Empire displaying roads, forts, settlements, towns, and provincial boundaries.
  • ORBIS: The Stanford Geospatial Network Model of the Roman World (Stanford University) - An interactive simulation tool that calculates the financial expense, transit time, and seasonal logistics of traveling across the Roman road and maritime network.

Classical Art, Pottery & Numismatics

Visual archives, ceramic typologies, and coin catalogues documenting Greco-Roman material culture.

  • Beazley Archive Pottery Database (Classical Art Research Centre / University of Oxford) - The world's largest research repository and image database dedicated to ancient Greek painted and figure-decorated pottery.
  • Online Coins of the Roman Empire (American Numismatic Society) - A comprehensive, linked open data catalogue documenting every imperial Roman coin type from Augustus to Zeno, linked to high-resolution museum specimens.
  • Roman Provincial Coinage Online (Ashmolean Museum / University of Oxford) - The definitive digital research catalogue for civic and provincial coin issues produced across the Roman provinces from 44 BCE onward.
  • Nomisma.org (American Numismatic Society) - A collaborative linked open data initiative providing stable digital definitions, ontologies, and identifier URIs for ancient numismatic concepts and mints.

Scholarly Discovery, Book Reviews & Open Bibliographies

Open journals and research discovery platforms for navigating classical scholarship without subscription barriers.

  • Bryn Mawr Classical Review (Bryn Mawr College) - The oldest and most comprehensive open-access, peer-reviewed journal publishing evaluations of new monographs in classical studies, history, and classical archaeology.
  • AWOL: The Ancient World Online (Charles E. Jones) - A widely consulted research discovery clearinghouse indexing open-access books, journals, databases, and digital humanities projects across the ancient Mediterranean.
